Turnstile privacy notice

Heaven Hotel uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help protect login, registration and password-reset forms from automated abuse. The check runs in the background.

Cloudflare processes browser and device signals to perform the check. Our server sends the resulting verification token and your IP address to Cloudflare to validate it.

For this processing, the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum applies. See also Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.
